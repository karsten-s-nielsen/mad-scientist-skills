# Sources and provenance

The metric anti-patterns E1–E6 are from Rahimian (2026); **E7 (a metric on unobserved positions /
observability) and the review-reliability guidance are our own observability extension and accumulated
project post-mortems**, not the base paper. Everything in this skill is stated so it can be lifted into any
repo; it names no private data and quotes no restricted numbers.

- **Rahimian, P. (2026).** *Auditing Construct Validity in Agentic Decision Support with Sports Analytics Case
  Study.* KDD Workshop on Evaluation and Trustworthiness of Agentic AI (KDD-WS-AgenticEval '26), ACM.
  Anti-pattern anchors cited therein: Bauer & Anzer 2021 (E1); Forcher et al. 2022 (E2, self-acknowledged);
  Merckx et al. 2021 (E3); Calabuig et al. 2024 (E4); Morgan 2018 (E5, self-acknowledged); Andrienko et al.
  2017 (E6). ⚠ Cite Rahimian (2026) for the **taxonomy only** — the "% of studies that ignore block
  type / validate on turnovers" figures are in the repo's unpublished notes, not the paper.
- **Landis, J. R., & Koch, G. G. (1977).** The measurement of observer agreement for categorical data.
  *Biometrics* 33(1). (κ interpretation bands.)
- The validation ladder, the plant / scramble / ceiling controls, crossed-effects attribution, the
  coordinate-frame pre-flight, and the pre-ship defect list are **in-house lessons** from prior cycles; use
  them as principles.
